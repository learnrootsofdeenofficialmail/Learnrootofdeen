Study Notes

Shuru karne se pehle yaad rakhein

Chhote practical tips jo har student ke kaam aayen.

Ek waqt mein ek cheez

  • Ek lesson ko 2 se 3 baar padhein, phir aage badhein.
  • Roz 15 se 20 minute bhi kaafi hain agar consistent hon.
  • Jo samajh na aaye use notebook mein likh lein.

Sources ka adab

  • Quran ka tarjuma aur tafseer alag alag hote hain, dono dekhein.
  • Hadees ki sehat (sanad) ke liye hadees ki kitab ke muhaqqiq ka hawala dekhein.
  • Masle mein apne maslak ya kisi qualified aalim se pooch lein.
